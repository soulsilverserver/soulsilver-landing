The guarantee

The target is in the contract.

Anyone can promise “more reach”. We state a specific number in the quote: how many qualified leads arrive within 30 days. It goes into the contract, and from then on you can hold us to it.

What we commit to

The lead target set out in the contract, within 30 days. We set that target after the assessment, based on your industry and your ad budget — not a generic figure, but yours.

If we fall short

We keep the campaign running free of charge until the target is met. We charge no further management fee for that period. The ad budget still runs on your side.

What it asks of you

The guarantee only holds if the conditions of the campaign don't change midway. So we set four conditions:

  1. The agreed minimum ad budget runs for the whole period.
  2. You call leads back within 4 working hours — a fresh enquiry is worth something, a three-day-old one isn't.
  3. The advertised offer and price level don't change during the campaign.
  4. You grant access to the ad accounts and to the tracking.
Definition

What does “qualified” mean?

Without this the guarantee would be an empty sentence. Four conditions, and all four must be met — this definition is part of the contract.

Real contact details

They gave a phone number or an e-mail address and confirmed it. A wrong number is not a lead.

In your area

Your service and your catchment area. We don't count anything you can't actually serve.

A specific need

They stated what they want and by when. “Just browsing” isn't enough.

They saw the price

The ad or the landing page showed the starting price — so the price isn't a surprise on the call.

Doesn't count: wrong phone number · the same person twice · competitors and suppliers · job seekers · outside your catchment area · enquiries with no specific need.

Limits

Who it's for — and who it isn't.

It's for you if
  • you're a local service provider, contractor or trade business
  • the value of one job can carry the advertising cost
  • you have the capacity to take on more work
  • someone is there to call enquiries back within 4 working hours
It's not for you if
  • your calendar is already full and you can't take on more
  • the value of the service is too small for the advertising cost
  • you can only respond to leads days later
  • you expect a whole year's results from a single month

If the assessment shows it isn't worth starting, we'll say so. Launching a campaign without the capacity to serve it is worse than not launching: it produces frustrated enquirers and bad reviews.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What if the lead target in the contract isn't met?

We keep the campaign running free of charge until the target is met. We charge no further management fee for that period. The ad budget still runs on your side.

Why do you guarantee the lead and not the signed job?

The lead is what we deliver: the campaign, the landing page and the tracking are our work. Whether a job gets signed also depends on how fast you call the enquirer back and how you negotiate. We don't promise what we don't control — so there's nothing to argue about on day 30.

How much ad budget does it take?

We work it out at the capacity assessment, because it depends on the industry and the catchment area. The management fee is on top of the ad budget, and you pay that budget directly to the platform from your own account.

What counts as a qualified lead?

Four conditions must be met together: they gave real, confirmed contact details; they're within your service area and catchment; they stated a specific need; and they saw the starting price before getting in touch. The exact definition is part of the contract.

What if I don't have capacity for more work?

Then this offer isn't for you, and we'll tell you that at the assessment. Launching a campaign when you can't serve the leads is worse than not launching: it produces frustrated enquirers and bad reviews.
