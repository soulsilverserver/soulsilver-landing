Cookie policy

Effective: 2 September 2026

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small data file that a website places in your browser. It lets the site recognise a returning visitor, remember settings and produce traffic statistics. On their own, cookies cannot directly identify you personally.

2. Which cookies do we use?

TypeGoalConsent
Essential cookiesIndispensable to the operation of the site and to remembering your cookie choice (for example, storing that decision in your browser).No consent required
Statistical / analytics cookiesMeasure traffic and site usage anonymously (Google measurement services).Subject to consent
Marketing cookiesMeasuring advertising performance and serving relevant ads (Google Ads).Subject to consent

The site currently runs Google services (Google Ads / measurement, gtag). These cookies are activated when you click “Accept” in the cookie bar.

3. Managing your consent

On your first visit a cookie bar appears where you can choose “Accept” or “Decline”. Your browser stores that decision (in local storage), so we don't ask again on your next visit.

To change an earlier decision, clear the stored data for this site in your browser — the cookie bar will then appear again.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you refuse cookies altogether, or delete cookies already stored. The exact steps differ by browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) — look for “Settings → Privacy / Cookies”. Note: if you block essential cookies, some functions of the site will not work properly.

5. Further information

You can read more about how personal data is handled in our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions, write to info@soulsilvermarketing.com.
