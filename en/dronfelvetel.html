FAQ

Frequently asked.

Do you need a special permit to fly a drone?

In certain areas, yes. We handle the required permits and regulatory compliance, so you don't have to deal with it.

How long until the video is ready?

The Basic package can be ready within 48 hours; turnaround on larger projects depends on the length — we agree that up front.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If the weather isn't suitable for safe flying, we arrange a new date free of charge.
