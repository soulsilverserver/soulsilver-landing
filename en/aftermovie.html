FAQ

Frequently asked.

How long does it take?

A quick teaser can be ready within 48 hours; the turnaround on the full aftermovie depends on the length and the volume of footage — we agree that up front.

Can we also get a shorter social media version?

Yes. Alongside the full film we produce a shorter Reels/Shorts/TikTok version, optimised for social media.

Do you cover multi-day events?

Yes, we shoot multi-day events too. In that case we adapt the crew and the delivery schedule to how the event runs.
