FAQ

Frequently asked.

How many products do you shoot in a day?

That depends on the size of the products and how many set-ups you want — several dozen packshots can be done in a day; we agree the exact number up front.

Does the shoot happen at your place or mine?

Whichever suits you: we shoot in the studio with controlled lighting, or at your location — we'll recommend an approach based on the product and the goal.

Do I get editable/retouched files?

Yes — we deliver the finished, retouched images at sizes optimised for your platforms, ready to use straight away.
